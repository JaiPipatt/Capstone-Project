\section{Module CAD Design}
\label{sec:module-cad-design}

The outer mechanical design is based on the SMORES-EP modular robot developed by the ModLab at the University of Pennsylvania \cite{smores_ep}. The original SMORES-EP module is approximately \(80\) mm in size. For our prototype, the main dimensions were adapted to \(100\) mm to provide more space for the required mechanical and electronic components.

\subsection{Outer Wheels}

Two independently driven outer wheels were designed based on the differential-drive arrangement of SMORES-EP. Each wheel has a diameter of \(100\) mm and includes a groove for an NBR N70 O-ring with a cross-section of approximately \(1.5\) mm, which is used as the rubber tire. Space for the EP-magnet docking mechanism is also provided in the wheel. Mounting posts were added to provide a mounting location for the PCB that will generate the electrical pulse for switching the EP-magnet coil.

\subsection{Pan Face}

The pan face was designed based on the SMORES-EP pan mechanism. It has a diameter of \(90\) mm and does not include the O-ring groove used on the driving wheels. Space for the EP-magnet docking mechanism is included in the face.

\subsection{Tilt Face}

The tilt face was adapted from the SMORES-EP tilt mechanism. The outer rectangular part has dimensions of approximately \(90 \times 71.6\) mm and includes space for the EP-magnet docking mechanism. An additional motor mount was added to accommodate the motor used in our prototype.

\section{Preparation for Phase B: Magnet}
\label{sec:phase-b-magnet}

The magnetic docking system is based on the Electro-Permanent Magnet EPM connector developed for SMORES-EP \cite{ep_face}. The original SMORES-EP module has a characteristic length of approximately \(80\) mm, and its EP-Face uses four EP magnets arranged in a ring on the connecting face. Each EP magnet consists of an AlNiCo 5 cylindrical magnet and an NdFeB magnet placed side by side, two low-carbon steel pole pieces, and a copper coil wound around the magnetic assembly. The original EP-Face uses magnet cylinders \(4.76\) mm in diameter and \(9.53\) mm in length, with the four-magnet arrangement fitting within a \(42\) mm diameter circular region. The magnets protrude approximately \(0.5\) mm from the connecting face. The copper coil uses 30 AWG wire with 200 turns \cite{ep_face}. For our \(100\) mm prototype, this arrangement and its associated dimensions serve as the design reference and are being adapted to fit our larger face and mechanical structure.

The operating principle relies on the contrasting magnetic properties of \textbf{AlNiCo 5} and \textbf{NdFeB}. AlNiCo 5 has relatively low coercivity, meaning its magnetization direction can be reversed by an external magnetic field. NdFeB, in contrast, has high coercivity and retains its magnetization under the same field pulse. This difference allows the two magnets to work together as a switchable permanent-magnet system.

A short electrical pulse is sent through the \textbf{copper coil}, generating a magnetic field around the magnet assembly. This pulse reverses the magnetization direction of the AlNiCo 5 while the NdFeB remains magnetized in its original direction. When the two magnetic fields are aligned, they reinforce each other and the resulting magnetic flux is guided through the \textbf{steel pole pieces} toward the connecting face. This produces the \textbf{ON state}, generating an attractive force with a mating EP connector.

When a pulse is applied in the opposite direction, the AlNiCo 5 magnetization reverses while the NdFeB remains unchanged. The two magnetic fields then oppose each other, reducing the magnetic flux reaching the connecting face. This produces the \textbf{OFF state}, allowing the modules to separate. Since the magnetic state remains after the pulse, continuous power is not required to maintain the ON or OFF state.

The original EP-Face uses AlNiCo 5, NdFeB, ASTM 1018 low-carbon steel pole pieces, and a copper coil with 30 AWG wire and 200 turns. For our prototype, the selected materials are:

\begin{itemize}
\item AlNiCo 5 rod magnet
\item NdFeB magnet
\item Mild steel 1018 / S20C
\item 30 AWG enamelled copper wire
\end{itemize}

The EP-magnet dimensions, coil winding parameters, and driving circuit will be finalized through fabrication and testing in Phase B.
